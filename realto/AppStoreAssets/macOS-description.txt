Realto turns raw property walkthrough footage into a branded, ready-to-post listing video — right on your Mac, in under 5 minutes.

Built for real estate agents and photographers who need professional-looking video without hiring an editor, learning a timeline-based editing tool, or paying a monthly subscription for something they'll use a few times a week.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Drag in your walkthrough clips (or photos) from Finder.
2. Pick a template — Classic, Bold, or Minimal.
3. Enter the property details: address, price, beds/baths, square footage.
4. Add your branding once — name, phone, brokerage, logo — and it's saved for every future listing.
5. Choose a music track from the bundled, licensed library.
6. Preview the real, finished composition — no surprises after export.
7. Export in 9:16, 1:1, and 16:9 simultaneously, ready for Instagram, feed posts, and YouTube.

WHY REALTO
• No account, no login, no phone number required. Open the app and start your first video immediately.
• 100% on-device processing. Nothing uploads and nothing requires a connection — export a video from a house with no signal.
• Reliable rendering. Exports never fail silently — if something goes wrong, you'll know immediately, with a clear retry.
• Branding that sticks. Set your agent profile once; every new listing reuses it automatically.
• Multiple templates so your videos don't look like everyone else's.
• Bundled, licensed music — no hunting for tracks you're legally allowed to use in a client's marketing video.

PRICING

PRIVACY
