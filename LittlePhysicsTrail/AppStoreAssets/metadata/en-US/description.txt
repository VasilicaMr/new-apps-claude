Why does a big ship float when a tiny coin sinks? What does a magnet pull, and what does it ignore? Little Physics Trail turns big science ideas into short, playful sorting games for curious kids.

Follow the owl along a winding trail. Each stop starts with one simple big idea, read aloud, then your child drags everyday things into the right group. Every answer opens a fact card that explains why, so kids don't just guess. They learn the reason.

EIGHT STOPS, EIGHT BIG IDEAS
• Sink or Float: why some things float and others sink
• Magnet Magic: which things a magnet pulls
• Push or Pull: forces all around us
• Light & Shadow: what makes its own light
• Hot & Cold: heat in everyday life
• Loud & Quiet: how sound works
• Down the Ramp: things that roll and things that slide
• Solid, Liquid, Gas: the states of matter

Each stop has ten items to sort, from rubber ducks and paper clips to drums, ice cubes and balloons.

MADE FOR LITTLE HANDS
• Drag an item to its group, or just tap the group
• Kind feedback: a wrong guess just bounces back to try again
• Read-aloud narration for kids who are still learning to read
• Earn up to three stars on every stop and play again to beat your best
• Bright, friendly design with a happy celebration at the end of each stop

LEARNING THAT STICKS
Kids learn science best by sorting, testing and asking "why?". Every item comes with a short, clear explanation in kid-friendly words, so playing builds real understanding of the physics around them, at the bath, in the kitchen and on the playground.
