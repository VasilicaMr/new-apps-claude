# Curriculum

Audience: ages **4–8** (pre-K to grade 2). The ideas line up with early-years science standards
such as NGSS K-PS2 (pushes and pulls), K-PS3 (sunlight and warming), 1-PS4 (sound and light) and
2-PS1 (properties of matter). Wording is kept simple: short sentences, everyday objects, and
everything read aloud.

Each stop teaches through three steps:

1. **Big idea**: one headline, a short explanation, and a list of the bins.
2. **Sort and explain**: 10 items, each with a one- or two-sentence "why".
3. **Try at home**: a safe, cheap experiment with a grown-up.

---

## 1 · Sink or Float 🛁 (buoyancy and density)

- **Goal:** predict whether an object floats from how heavy it is *for its size*, not just how heavy it is.
- **Surprises:** 🚢 a ship is heavy metal but floats, because its hollow shape is full of air. 🪙 a coin is tiny but sinks.
- **Try at home:** a bowl of water; guess, then test a spoon, a cork and an orange.
- **Ask your child:** "Why do you think the big ship floats but the little coin sinks?"

## 2 · Magnet Magic 🧲 (magnetism)

- **Goal:** magnets attract iron and steel; most other materials are not attracted.
- **Surprise:** 📄 magnets don't pull paper, but they pull *through* it, which is how fridge magnets work.
- **Try at home:** a magnet hunt around the kitchen.
- **Note for grown-ups:** not every metal is magnetic (aluminium foil, copper, most coins). The items avoid those on purpose; it's a good follow-up chat for older kids.

## 3 · Push or Pull 💪 (forces)

- **Goal:** a force is a push or a pull; forces start, stop and change motion.
- **Examples:** everyday actions such as a shopping cart, kite string, doorbell, socks and pulling a carrot.
- **Try at home:** push a toy car gently, then hard. Which goes farther?
- **Ask:** "Is opening a door a push or a pull?" (It depends which side you're on.)

## 4 · Light & Shadow 💡 (light sources and reflection)

- **Goal:** tell apart things that **make** light from things that only **reflect** it; shadows happen when light is blocked.
- **Surprises:** 🌙 the Moon makes no light of its own. 🪞 a mirror and 💎 a diamond only bounce light.
- **Try at home:** flashlight shadow puppets.

## 5 · Hot & Cold 🌡️ (heat transfer)

- **Goal:** heat is energy that moves from hotter to colder things.
- **Hook:** ice melting in a warm hand, blowing on soup.
- **Try at home:** hold an ice cube for a moment. Your hand gets cold while the ice melts.

## 6 · Loud & Quiet 🔊 (sound as vibration)

- **Goal:** sound is made by vibrations; bigger vibrations are louder.
- **Try at home:** fingers on the throat while humming to feel the vibration.

## 7 · Down the Ramp 🛝 (gravity, rolling and friction)

- **Goal:** gravity pulls things down a slope; round things roll, flat things slide, and friction slows sliding.
- **Surprise:** 🛹 a skateboard is flat, but it rolls because its wheels are round.
- **Try at home:** book-and-pillow ramp; race a ball against a block.

## 8 · Solid, Liquid, Gas 🧪 (states of matter)

- **Goal:** classify everyday matter as solid, liquid or gas, and know that water can be all three.
- **Model used:** "tiny bits" (particles) that hold tight, slide around or zoom everywhere.
- **Careful choices:** 🎈 and 🫧 are labelled "Air in a balloon" / "Air in bubbles", because the
  balloon itself is a solid.
- **Try at home:** watch an ice cube melt; with a grown-up, look at steam from a pot.

---

## Accuracy rules for new content

- Prefer items with **one clear answer**. If it "depends" (a door, a yo-yo, a cloud), leave it out
  or name the item precisely ("Air in a balloon").
- Facts must be **true, not just simple**. Simplify the vocabulary, not the science.
- Every experiment must be safe with ordinary household items and **grown-up supervision** where heat is involved.
