How much do you really keep after fuel, the car and the app's cut? DriveCap is a logbook for ride-hailing and taxi drivers that turns your earnings screenshots into clear numbers: what you made, what it cost, and what you keep per hour and per km.

READ EARNINGS FROM A SCREENSHOT
• Take a screenshot of a trip, a day or a week in your driving app
• DriveCap reads the fares, tips, bonus, trips, kilometers and destination for you
• When you end a shift, it goes straight to reading your earnings screenshot
• With Pro, it finds new earnings screenshots by itself and shows them on Home, ready to add with one tap

TIME YOUR SHIFTS
• Start and end a shift with one tap
• See the shift timer on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island
• Hands-free with Siri: "Start my shift in DriveCap"

COSTS WITHOUT TYPING
• Photograph a fuel or service receipt and DriveCap fills in the category, total, liters, station and date
• Keep every expense in one diary, with the receipt photo

YOUR REAL NUMBERS
• Net after costs for the day, week, month or year
• Earnings and profit per hour and per km
• Compare apps and see where your money goes, by category

EARN SMARTER (PRO)
• Your minimum fare: the true cost of your car per km, so you know which trips lose money
• Weekly goal: the hours you still need to reach what you want to keep
• Best times: the days and hours that pay best for you

JUST ASK
Ask "How much did I make this week?" or type "fuel $40 38 L" and DriveCap answers or adds it for you. Uses Apple Intelligence on supported iPhones and still understands the common questions without it.

CAR PAPERS ON TIME
Reminders for the technical inspection, insurance, road vignette and oil change, by date or by kilometers, with a notice 30, 7 and 1 day before.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account and no sign-up
• Everything you log stays on your iPhone
• Screenshots and receipts are read on the iPhone and never sent anywhere
• Export a CSV for your accountant or a backup (Pro)

FREE AND PRO
DriveCap is free to use for shifts, manual entries, stats, receipts, reminders and the assistant, with 10 screenshot reads a month.
DriveCap Pro adds unlimited screenshot reading, screenshots found for you, Earn smarter and CSV export. Choose a monthly or yearly subscription, both with a 7-day free trial, or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel them in your Apple Account settings.
